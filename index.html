Questions

Asked before booking.

How do I book?

Tap any “Book” button. It opens our link page, where you can message us directly. Tell us what you want and when, and we will confirm a time.

It’s my first Moroccan bath. What happens?

You relax in warm steam, then we apply black soap, let it rest and rinse it off. Next comes a full-body scrub with a kessa glove, which lifts away dead skin. A clay mask and a final rinse finish it. We explain every step as we go.

Can I combine treatments?

Yes. Bath then massage, or bath then facial, works especially well because the skin is freshly exfoliated. Ask us to plan the order for you.

Do you have packages?

Yes, including packages for brides, Eid and special occasions. Check the Packages highlight on our Instagram, or message us for the current offers.

What should I bring?

Nothing special. Arrive a few minutes early so you are not rushed, and let us know about allergies or sensitive skin before we start.

What are your prices and opening hours?

They change with offers and seasons, so message us on Instagram for today’s prices and free times. We usually reply quickly.

Your hour is waiting.

The steam is easy to turn on. The hard part is picking a day. Pick one now and let us handle the rest.
